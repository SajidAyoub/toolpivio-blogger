Estimate break-even sales units from fixed costs, selling price and variable unit cost. See the contribution-margin formula and whole-unit rounding.

How to use this tool

  1. Enter fixed costs for one defined period or project.
  2. Enter selling price per unit and variable cost per unit in the same currency.
  3. Calculate the units needed to cover costs. Use the rounded-up whole-unit result when selling indivisible products.

Formula and units

Break-even units = fixed costs ÷ (selling price per unit − variable cost per unit), rounded upward.

Worked example

Fixed costs of 1,000, a selling price of 25 and variable cost of 15 require 100 units to break even.

What to check before using the result

Assumes constant unit price and costs and that all units sell. A price at or below variable cost has no finite break-even point. Taxes and changes in capacity are excluded.

What if variable cost is at least the selling price?

There is no positive contribution available to cover fixed costs. A finite break-even quantity cannot be calculated under that pricing model.
