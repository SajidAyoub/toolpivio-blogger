How to use this tool

  1. Enter advertising spend in your chosen currency.
  2. Enter the positive whole-number count of impressions for the same reporting period.
  3. Calculate the cost of one thousand impressions; impressions count ad displays, not unique people.

Formula and units

CPM = advertising spend ÷ impressions × 1,000.

Worked example

A spend of 1,000 and 200,000 impressions gives a CPM of 5.

What to check before using the result

Use served impressions rather than reach. Repeated displays count as separate impressions; viewable CPM may use a different denominator.

Are impressions the same as reach?

No. One person may see an ad several times. CPM uses impressions, while reach estimates distinct people or accounts.
