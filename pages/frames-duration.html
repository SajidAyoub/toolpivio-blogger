How to use this tool

  1. Choose frames-to-duration or duration-to-frames.
  2. Enter the frame count or time in seconds, then enter the frame rate used by your project.
  3. Calculate the conversion and check whether whole-frame rounding is suitable for your timeline.

Formula and units

Duration in seconds = total frames ÷ frame rate. Frames = duration × frame rate, rounded to the nearest whole frame.

Worked example

300 frames at 30 frames per second last 10 seconds. At 29.97 fps, the same 300 frames last about 10.0100 seconds.

What to check before using the result

Uses a constant decimal frame rate and seconds, not drop-frame timecode. Enter the actual rate used by your project; 29.97 and 30 fps are different rates.

Does this convert SMPTE drop-frame timecode?

No. This converts a frame count and elapsed seconds using a constant frame rate. Drop-frame timecode is a separate labeling convention and does not remove video frames.
