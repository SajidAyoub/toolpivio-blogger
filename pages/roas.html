Calculate return on ad spend (ROAS) to understand how much revenue your advertising generates. Use revenue attributed to the same campaign and period as your spend.

How to calculate ROAS

Divide attributed revenue by advertising spend. For example, 5,000 in revenue ÷ 1,000 in spend = 5× ROAS, or 500%.

Use the same currency for both inputs. ROAS measures revenue efficiency; it does not account for product costs, overhead, or net profit.
