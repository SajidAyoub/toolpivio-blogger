How to use this tool

  1. Enter campaign revenue attributed using one consistent attribution model.
  2. Enter advertising spend for the same campaign and reporting period; spend must be greater than zero.
  3. Calculate to see the revenue multiple and percentage. Reset clears the form for another campaign.

Formula and units

ROAS = attributed revenue ÷ advertising spend. ROAS percentage = ROAS × 100.

Worked example

A campaign generates 5,000 in attributed revenue from 1,000 in ad spend. Its ROAS is 5,000 ÷ 1,000 = 5×, equivalent to 500%.

What to check before using the result

ROAS is a revenue measure. It excludes product costs, refunds, agency fees, platform fees and overhead unless those are included in your source figures. A high ROAS does not establish that a campaign is profitable.

Is 5× ROAS the same as a 500% profit?

No. 5× ROAS means five units of revenue for each unit of advertising spend. Calculate profit after all relevant costs separately with the profit margin calculator.
