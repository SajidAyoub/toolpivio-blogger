How to use this tool

  1. Enter advertising spend in one currency.
  2. Enter the positive whole-number count of clicks for the same campaign and period.
  3. Calculate the average cost per click, then compare it with the value of your traffic.

Formula and units

CPC = advertising spend ÷ clicks.

Worked example

A campaign spending 1,000 for 500 clicks has an average CPC of 2.

What to check before using the result

Enter spend and clicks from the same campaign, currency and reporting period. Average CPC does not predict an individual auction price.

Does a lower CPC always mean a better campaign?

No. Lower traffic cost can still produce fewer qualified leads or sales. Review conversion rate, CPA and ROAS alongside CPC.
