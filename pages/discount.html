Calculate a sale price and savings from an original price and percentage discount. Check the price before tax, shipping or other extra charges.

If the tool does not load, open it directly.

How to use this tool

  1. Enter the original price in a single currency.
  2. Enter the percentage discount from zero to one hundred.
  3. Calculate to see the discounted price and the amount saved.

Formula and units

Savings = original price × discount ÷ 100. Sale price = original price − savings.

Worked example

An 80 item with a 25% discount saves 20 and has a sale price of 60.

What to check before using the result

Accepts discounts from 0% to 100%. Tax, shipping, currency rounding and stacked promotions are excluded.

Can I add two percentage discounts together?

Successive discounts usually apply to the reduced price. A 20% discount followed by 10% gives a 28% total reduction, not 30%. Calculate each stage separately.
