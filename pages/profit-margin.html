How to calculate profit margin

Profit = revenue − cost. Profit margin = profit ÷ revenue × 100. For example, 100 revenue and 60 cost gives 40 profit and a 40% margin.

Use the same currency and period for both inputs. Costs above revenue produce a negative margin. Margin is different from markup, which divides profit by cost.
