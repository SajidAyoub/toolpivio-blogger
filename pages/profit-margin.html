How to use this tool

  1. Enter revenue in your chosen currency for one product, order or reporting period.
  2. Enter all costs relevant to that same scope.
  3. Calculate to see profit and margin. Reset clears the inputs and result.

Formula and units

Profit = revenue − total cost. Profit margin (%) = (revenue − total cost) ÷ revenue × 100.

Worked example

Revenue of 100 and total cost of 60 produce a profit of 40. Profit margin is 40 ÷ 100 × 100 = 40%.

What to check before using the result

The result reflects only the costs you enter. Include shipping, returns, advertising and platform fees when relevant. Revenue must be greater than zero; costs above revenue correctly produce a negative margin. This is a planning calculation, not tax or accounting advice.

How is profit margin different from markup?

Margin divides profit by revenue; markup divides profit by cost. A product that costs 60 and sells for 100 has a 40% margin and about 66.67% markup.
