How to use this tool

  1. Enter the final trimmed width and height in the selected physical unit.
  2. Enter the bleed required on each edge by your print supplier.
  3. Calculate the complete artwork size, and extend the background into the bleed area in your design software.

Formula and units

Artwork width = trim width + 2 × bleed per edge; the same formula applies to height.

Worked example

A 210 × 297 mm trim with 3 mm bleed on every edge needs 216 × 303 mm artwork.

What to check before using the result

This calculates dimensions only and does not edit artwork or add crop marks. Ask your printer about bleed, safe zones and binding allowances.

Is bleed the same as a safe text margin?

No. Bleed extends artwork beyond the trim edge. A safe margin keeps essential text and graphics inside the trim edge. Confirm both requirements with your printer.
