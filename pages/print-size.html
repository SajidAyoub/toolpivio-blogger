How to use this tool

  1. Choose your source unit and enter the image or print dimensions.
  2. Enter the intended image resolution in pixels per inch when converting to or from pixels.
  3. Calculate the equivalent dimensions, then check the final requirements with your print supplier.

Formula and units

Inches = millimeters ÷ 25.4. Pixels = inches × pixels per inch (PPI).

Worked example

At 300 PPI, an image measuring 3,000 × 2,400 pixels converts to 10 × 8 inches or 254 × 203.2 mm.

What to check before using the result

PPI relates image pixels to physical size and is distinct from a printer’s ink-dot DPI. This converter does not resample an image. Pixel results show exact values and nearest whole pixels.

Why does the converter ask for PPI?

Millimeters and inches are physical units. Pixels describe a digital grid. PPI links that grid to a physical print size; without it there is no single pixel-to-inch conversion.
