How to use this tool

  1. Enter the clicks recorded by your chosen advertising or analytics platform.
  2. Enter impressions measured using the same campaign and reporting window.
  3. Calculate the percentage of impressions that resulted in clicks.

Formula and units

CTR (%) = clicks ÷ impressions × 100.

Worked example

500 clicks from 20,000 impressions produces a 2.5% click-through rate.

What to check before using the result

Use consistent click and impression definitions. Repeated clicks can make this arithmetic ratio exceed 100%; compare like-for-like reporting metrics.

Can I compare CTR across every advertising channel?

Use caution. Placements, audience intent and click definitions differ. Compare similar campaigns and confirm the platform's reporting definitions.
