How to use this tool

  1. Choose the conversion you want to measure, such as a purchase or qualified lead.
  2. Enter campaign spend and the number of those conversions for the same reporting window.
  3. Calculate the average cost of each recorded acquisition or action.

Formula and units

CPA = advertising spend ÷ attributed acquisitions.

Worked example

A spend of 1,000 for 50 acquisitions produces a CPA of 20.

What to check before using the result

Define the conversion event before comparing campaigns. This advertising metric excludes fulfillment costs and attribution outside your chosen reporting window.

Is CPA the same as customer acquisition cost?

Not necessarily. CPA often measures one advertising action. Customer acquisition cost may include sales salaries, software and other acquisition costs in addition to advertising.
