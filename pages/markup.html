How to use this tool

  1. Enter the cost of the item or service, using the same cost basis throughout.
  2. Enter the selling price in the same currency.
  3. Calculate profit and the percentage markup on cost. Selling below cost produces a negative markup.

Formula and units

Markup (%) = (selling price − unit cost) ÷ unit cost × 100.

Worked example

Buying an item for 60 and selling it for 100 produces 40 profit and 66.6667% markup.

What to check before using the result

Markup uses cost as the denominator; profit margin uses revenue. Include the costs relevant to your decision. Selling below cost produces a negative markup.

Why does a 50% markup give less than a 50% margin?

Markup is calculated on cost; margin is calculated on selling price. A cost of 100 marked up by 50% sells for 150, so the margin is 50 ÷ 150 = 33.33%.
