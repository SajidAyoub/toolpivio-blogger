Estimate horizontal and vertical image resolution from pixel dimensions and physical print size. This is pixels per inch (PPI), commonly called print DPI. It does not measure a printer’s ink dots.

How to calculate print resolution

Divide each pixel dimension by its print dimension in inches. For example, 3,000 × 2,400 pixels printed at 10 × 8 inches gives 300 PPI on both axes. Centimeters are converted using 2.54 cm per inch.

Around 300 PPI is a common starting point for close-viewed prints; requirements depend on the printer, material, and viewing distance. Different horizontal and vertical values may indicate an aspect-ratio mismatch. Confirm cropping or stretching with your printer.
