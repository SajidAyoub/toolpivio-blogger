Contact workspace and optional message delivery

Contact feature update: November 1, 2026. Message delivery is available only after the owner connects a real endpoint. The contact workspace displays its current sending status. With no connected endpoint, it cannot submit messages; the site owner does not receive your draft. A draft stays in the current page’s memory until you clear it, reload or leave. ToolPivio does not save that draft in cookies or browser storage. Choosing Copy draft places its text on your device clipboard; clear the clipboard separately when finished on a shared device.

If the owner later connects a Formspree form and you choose to submit with consent, your name, email address, reason, message, optional tool/page URL and consent are transferred to Formspree for processing and to the owner’s configured inbox. Formspree may process connection information, such as your IP address and browser information, and store submissions under its Privacy Policy and the form owner’s account settings. Messages may also remain in the recipient inbox. No fixed provider retention period is promised here. Do not send passwords, tokens, private files or sensitive personal or financial information.

The contact form sends only the fields you submit; it does not attach calculator values or image/PDF files. A provider acceptance response does not establish that a message reached an inbox or that a reply will be sent. If a request fails or cannot be confirmed, the draft remains in the page and the client does not retry the submission automatically.
